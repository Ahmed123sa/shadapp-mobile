FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ShadApp is the mobile workspace where clients and their account managers get
business done together — in one thread instead of scattered across email,
messaging apps and paperwork.

Every client gets a workspace. Inside it sit the conversation, the documents,
the contract, the meeting schedule and the payment record, all in one place and
all up to date for everyone who can see them.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

Talk to your account manager
• Message in a dedicated workspace chat, with replies, edits and read receipts
• Attach documents and photos straight from the conversation
• Flag a message as needing action so nothing gets lost in the scroll

Handle contracts and approvals
• Read a contract clause by clause on your phone
• Approve, reject, or ask for an edit — with your reason recorded
• See exactly what is waiting on you and what is waiting on the other side

Sign without printing anything
• Draw your signature on screen, type it, or upload an image
• Signed documents are stored in the workspace and available to both sides

Keep documents in order
• Upload contracts, licences, receipts and supporting files
• Your account manager can request specific documents; you see what is still
  outstanding
• Everything stays attached to the client it belongs to

Track payments
• See what has been requested, what is scheduled, what is overdue and what is
  settled
• Upload proof of payment — a transfer receipt or an invoice
• Follow installment schedules without chasing anyone for a statement
• ShadApp records payments; it never asks for your card or bank details

Never miss a meeting
• Schedule meetings, view them in a calendar, mark them complete or cancelled
• Push notifications for new messages, payment requests, approvals and meetings

FOR ACCOUNT MANAGERS

ShadApp is built for both sides of the relationship. Account managers get a
portfolio view across every client they handle: onboarding progress, pending
approvals, outstanding documents and payment status at a glance. Reports break
down accepted payments, client activity and team performance. Site visits can be
checked in on location, and an audit log records who changed, approved or signed
what, and when.

Organizations can add sub-users so a client's own team members work in the same
workspace with their own logins and their own permissions.

ARABIC AND ENGLISH

The whole app is available in Arabic and English, with full right-to-left
layout. Switch language any time in settings.

ACCESS

ShadApp is a business tool, not a public sign-up service. Your account is created
by your organization or your service provider, who will give you your login.

BEFORE YOU PASTE THIS IN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Replace [YOUR DOMAIN] in the last line with your real privacy policy URL,
   or delete those two lines. The policy URL is required elsewhere in the
   Console regardless.

2. Play strips or renders formatting differently than it looks here. The
   bullet character (•) and the ALL-CAPS headings are safe and commonly used.
   Do not add emoji to the short description.

3. Because sign-up is not public, Google Play REQUIRES demo credentials for
   review. Add them under App content > App access > "All or some
   functionality is restricted", with a working username, password and any
   steps a reviewer needs. Submissions that skip this are routinely rejected.

4. Claims to keep out of the listing (Play policy): rankings such as "#1" or
   "best", testimonials, price or discount claims, and anything the app does
   not actually do yet.

5. Also required for a first submission: app icon (512x512), feature graphic
   (1024x500), at least 2 phone screenshots, content rating questionnaire,
   target audience, data safety form, and the account deletion URL.
