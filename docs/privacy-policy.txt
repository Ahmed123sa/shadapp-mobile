1. WHO WE ARE
================================================================================

ShadApp is a business workspace app. Clients, their sub-users, and account
managers use it to exchange messages and documents, agree contracts, schedule
meetings, sign, and track payments.

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we", "us") operates the ShadApp mobile app and the backend
service it connects to, and is the controller of the personal data described in
this policy. This policy covers the iOS and Android apps and the ShadApp API
they talk to. It does not cover any separate website, or the services of a third
party you reach through a link in the app.

Accounts in ShadApp are created and administered by an organization, not by the
general public. If your employer or a service provider gave you your login, they
decide what business records are held about you in the app; we handle that data
on the terms set out below.

2. SUMMARY
================================================================================

The detail follows, but in short:

  Do we sell your data?
      No. Never, to anyone, for any purpose.

  Do we show ads or track you across apps?
      No. There is no advertising SDK and no cross-app tracking in ShadApp.

      Yes. HTTPS and secure WebSockets.

  Can you get your data deleted?
      Yes - see sections 10 and 11.

3. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
================================================================================

3.1 ACCOUNT AND PROFILE

Your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, chosen language, and the
role your organization assigned you (client, sub-user, or account manager). Your
password is never stored in readable form - it is verified against a one-way
hash on our server.

3.2 BUSINESS RECORDS YOU OR YOUR ACCOUNT MANAGER ENTER

Client and company details held in your workspace: company name, contact person,
email, phone, country, industry, business address, client type, account status,
and - where your organization records it - a date of birth for the named
contact.

3.3 MESSAGES, FILES AND SIGNATURES

Messages you send in a workspace chat, including edits, replies, and read
status. Files and photos you upload - contracts, supporting documents, and
anything you attach from your camera, photo library, or device storage.
Signature images, whether you draw them, type them, or upload an image of one.

3.4 MEETINGS AND APPROVALS

Meetings you schedule, attend, cancel or complete, and the approval decisions
you record against documents and messages - including who approved or rejected
an item, when, and any reason given.

3.5 PAYMENT RECORDS

Payment amounts and currency (SAR by default), due dates, installment schedules,
notes, status, and proof-of-payment files you upload such as a transfer receipt
or invoice.

    THE APP NEVER COLLECTS CARD OR BANK CREDENTIALS. There is no payment form,
    no card entry field, and no payment processor SDK in ShadApp. It records
    that a payment was requested or made; the payment itself happens outside
    the app.

3.6 LOCATION - CHECK-IN ONLY

Account managers can record where a client site visit took place. When you tap
"Check in", the app reads your device's current coordinates once and saves them
to that client's record. You can also set a client's location by dropping a pin
on a map instead, which reads no device location at all.

The app has no background location permission and no location tracking. Nothing
is read while the app is closed, and nothing is read unless you tap the button.
Denying the permission disables check-in; every other part of the app keeps
working.

3.7 DEVICE AND PUSH NOTIFICATION DATA

To deliver push notifications we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration
token, which identifies your app installation on your device. It is regenerated
when you reinstall the app, and deleted from our records when you sign out.

3.8 DIAGNOSTICS AND CRASH REPORTS

In released versions the app sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics: the
error and its stack trace, your device model, operating system version, and app
version. Crash collection is switched off entirely in development builds. Crash
reports are not used to build a profile of you and are not linked to your
messages or documents.

3.9 STORED ON YOUR DEVICE, NOT SENT TO US

Your session token is kept in the device's own secure store - the iOS Keychain
or the Android Keystore. Your name, email, avatar URL, role and language
preference are cached in ordinary app storage so screens can render before the
network responds. Signing out clears all of it.

4. PERMISSIONS THE APP REQUESTS
================================================================================

Every permission is requested at the moment it is needed and explained on
screen. You can refuse or withdraw any of them in your device settings.

  NOTIFICATIONS
      Why: Alerts for new messages, payment requests, approvals and meetings.
      If you refuse: The app works; you see updates only when you open it.

  LOCATION - WHILE IN USE
      Why: Recording the check-in location of a client site visit.
      If you refuse: Check-in is unavailable. Locations can still be set by
      map pin.

  CAMERA
      Why: Photographing a document to upload.
      If you refuse: You can still upload files already on your device.

  PHOTOS / FILES
      Why: Attaching documents, images and payment receipts.
      If you refuse: You cannot attach files.

  INTERNET
      Why: Required. The app is a client for an online service.

5. HOW WE USE INFORMATION
================================================================================

We use the information described above only to run the service:

  - To authenticate you and keep you signed in, and to enforce what your role
    is allowed to see and do.
  - To deliver messages, files, contracts, meetings, approvals and payment
    records between you and the people in your workspace.
  - To send push notifications about activity that concerns you.
  - To keep an audit record of significant actions - who changed, approved or
    signed what, and when - which is a core function of the service and cannot
    be switched off.
  - To diagnose crashes and fix defects.
  - To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations that apply to contract and
    payment records.

We do not use your data for advertising, for profiling, for training machine
learning models, or for any automated decision that produces a legal or
similarly significant effect on you.

9. SECURITY
================================================================================

  - All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS; realtime updates
    use a secure WebSocket connection.
  - Your session token is stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore,
    not in ordinary app storage.
  - Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  - Access to workspace data is restricted by role, and enforced on the server
    rather than only in the app.
  - Significant actions are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will
notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

================================================================================
