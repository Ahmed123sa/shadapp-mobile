Who we are

ShadApp is a business workspace app. Clients, their sub-users, and account managers use it to exchange messages and documents, agree contracts, schedule meetings, sign, and track payments.

[LEGAL ENTITY NAME] (“we”, “us”) operates the ShadApp mobile app and the backend service it connects to, and is the controller of the personal data described in this policy. This policy covers the iOS and Android apps and the ShadApp API they talk to. It does not cover any separate website, or the services of a third party you reach through a link in the app.

Accounts in ShadApp are created and administered by an organization, not by the general public. If your employer or a service provider gave you your login, they decide what business records are held about you in the app; we handle that data on the terms set out below.

Information we collect

Account and profile

Your name, email address, phone number, profile photo, chosen language, and the role your organization assigned you (client, sub-user, or account manager). Your password is never stored in readable form — it is verified against a one-way hash on our server.

Business records you or your account manager enter

Client and company details held in your workspace: company name, contact person, email, phone, country, industry, business address, client type, account status, and — where your organization records it — a date of birth for the named contact.

Messages, files and signatures

Messages you send in a workspace chat, including edits, replies, and read status. Files and photos you upload — contracts, supporting documents, and anything you attach from your camera, photo library, or device storage. Signature images, whether you draw them, type them, or upload an image of one.

Meetings and approvals

Meetings you schedule, attend, cancel or complete, and the approval decisions you record against documents and messages — including who approved or rejected an item, when, and any reason given.

Payment records

Payment amounts and currency (SAR by default), due dates, installment schedules, notes, status, and proof-of-payment files you upload such as a transfer receipt or invoice.

The app never collects card or bank credentials. There is no payment form, no card entry field, and no payment processor SDK in ShadApp. It records that a payment was requested or made; the payment itself happens outside the app.

Location — check-in only

Account managers can record where a client site visit took place. When you tap Check in, the app reads your device's current coordinates once and saves them to that client's record. You can also set a client's location by dropping a pin on a map instead, which reads no device location at all.

The app has no background location permission and no location tracking. Nothing is read while the app is closed, and nothing is read unless you tap the button. Denying the permission disables check-in; every other part of the app keeps working.

Device and push notification data

To deliver push notifications we collect a Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, which identifies your app installation on your device. It is regenerated when you reinstall the app, and deleted from our records when you sign out.

Diagnostics and crash reports

In released versions the app sends crash reports to Firebase Crashlytics: the error and its stack trace, your device model, operating system version, and app version. Crash collection is switched off entirely in development builds. Crash reports are not used to build a profile of you and are not linked to your messages or documents.

Stored on your device, not sent to us

Your session token is kept in the device's own secure store — the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore. Your name, email, avatar URL, role and language preference are cached in ordinary app storage so screens can render before the network responds. Signing out clears all of it.

How we use information

We use the information described above only to run the service:

  • To authenticate you and keep you signed in, and to enforce what your role is allowed to see and do.
  • To deliver messages, files, contracts, meetings, approvals and payment records between you and the people in your workspace.
  • To send push notifications about activity that concerns you.
  • To keep an audit record of significant actions — who changed, approved or signed what, and when — which is a core function of the service and cannot be switched off.
  • To diagnose crashes and fix defects.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations that apply to contract and payment records.

We do not use your data for advertising, for profiling, for training machine learning models, or for any automated decision that produces a legal or similarly significant effect on you.

Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS; realtime updates use a secure WebSocket connection.
  • Your session token is stored in the iOS Keychain or the Android Keystore, not in ordinary app storage.
  • Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • Access to workspace data is restricted by role, and enforced on the server rather than only in the app.
  • Significant actions are recorded in an audit log.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • Show you the personal data we hold about you, and provide a copy.
  • Correct anything inaccurate. Most profile fields you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Delete your data, subject to the legal retention periods in section 8.
  • Restrict or object to a particular use of your data.

You can also change these at any time without contacting us:

  • Notifications — turn them off in your device settings, or per-category in the app's settings screen.
  • Location — revoke the permission in your device settings; check-in stops working, nothing else does.
  • Camera and photos — revoke in device settings; you can still attach files from storage.

Write to privacy@[YOUR DOMAIN] to exercise any of these. We will respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first. If your account was created by an organization, we may need to route your request through them.

Deleting your account

To delete your ShadApp account and the personal data associated with it, email privacy@[YOUR DOMAIN] from the address on your account, or use the account deletion request page at https://[YOUR DOMAIN]/delete-account.

We will confirm the request, delete your profile, credentials, push tokens and cached device data, and remove or anonymize your messages. Contracts, signatures, payment records and audit entries are retained for the periods in section 8 where law requires it, and are then deleted.

Children

ShadApp is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
